# Usage Analytics

Track views, downloads, and engagement across all your galleries. Make data-driven decisions to grow your photography business.

## Understand Your Audience

See exactly how clients interact with your galleries. Know which photos get the most attention and use those insights to improve your work and marketing.

- **View tracking** - See how many times each gallery and photo has been viewed
- **Download metrics** - Track which photos clients download most
- **Engagement patterns** - Understand when clients are most active
- **Geographic insights** - See where your audience is located

## Gallery Performance

Compare performance across all your galleries to understand what resonates with your clients.

- **Gallery rankings** - See your top-performing galleries at a glance
- **Trend analysis** - Track how engagement changes over time
- **Client activity** - Monitor individual client interactions
- **Conversion tracking** - See which galleries lead to downloads and purchases
