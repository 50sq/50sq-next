# Gallery Management

Organize, curate, and deliver your photography work with our powerful gallery management system.

## Effortless Organization

50sq's gallery management puts you in complete control of how your photos are organized and presented. Create unlimited galleries, organize them into projects, and customize the layout to match your brand.

- **Drag-and-drop organization** - Rearrange photos instantly with intuitive drag-and-drop
- **Smart collections** - Automatically group photos by date, location, or custom tags
- **Nested galleries** - Create sub-galleries for complex projects like weddings or events
- **Bulk operations** - Select multiple photos to move, copy, or delete in one action

## Beautiful Presentation

Your work deserves to be showcased beautifully. Every gallery you create is designed to highlight your photography with clean, distraction-free layouts.

- **Multiple layout options** - Grid, masonry, slideshow, and more
- **Customizable cover images** - Choose the perfect hero image for each gallery
- **Color themes** - Match your gallery design to your brand colors
- **Mobile-optimized** - Galleries look stunning on any device

## Client Experience

Delivering photos to clients has never been easier. Share galleries with a single link and let clients browse, favorite, and download their photos.

- **Password protection** - Secure sensitive galleries with custom passwords
- **Expiring links** - Set automatic expiration dates for temporary access
- **Download controls** - Choose which resolution clients can download
- **Favorites collection** - Clients can mark their favorite photos for easy reference
